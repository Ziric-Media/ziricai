Built for people, not just businesses

Sarah App is the consumer product in the ZiricAI ecosystem. Personal Sarah helps with planning, questions, and tasks; Sarah Work delegates longer assignments; My Knowledge is your permissioned memory; Discover and My Organisations connect you to real businesses on the Sarah Network.

  • Available now Personal Sarah — Chat, home intelligence, and personal sessions on sarah.ziricai.com.
  • Available now Sarah Work — Delegate structured tasks with progress and reports.
  • Available now My Knowledge — Choose what Sarah may read and remember about you.
  • Available now Discover & organisations — Follow businesses and see updates in your feed.
  • Available now Status & social feed — Stay in touch with people and organisations you care about.
  • In development Sarah Pay & wallet — Payments and wallet features are in active development — not marketed as fully live until release.
  • Coming later Sarah Mobility — Ride and mobility integrations are on the strategic roadmap.
