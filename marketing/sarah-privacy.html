Sarah App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Sarah is the ZiricAI consumer super app for individuals — Personal Sarah chat, Work, My Knowledge, News, Discover, company chats, and Sarah Network. This policy applies when you use Sarah at sarah.ziricai.com or the official Sarah app distributed through Google Play (Trusted Web Activity). It does not replace the privacy terms of the ZiricAI Company Portal or business WhatsApp integrations; those are covered separately at ZiricAI Business Platform Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

MyPlayc (Pty) Ltd (“MyPlayc”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is the legal operator of the Sarah app listed on Google Play and is responsible for this privacy policy for the Sarah consumer service.

Sarah is part of the ZiricAI ecosystem. Software and AI services may be developed and hosted in partnership with Ziric Media and other ZiricAI group entities under contractual and technical arrangements. When you chat with a business on Discover, that business may be the data controller for information you share in that company’s Sarah conversation; MyPlayc and ZiricAI infrastructure typically act as processors to deliver those messages and tools on the business’s instructions.

By creating a Sarah account or using the app, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree, do not use Sarah.

2. What Sarah does (and what this policy covers)

Sarah lets you:

  • Talk to Personal Sarah and Work Sarah (AI-assisted chat, reminders, and tasks you choose to run)
  • Store structured information in My Knowledge so Sarah can personalise replies within your permission settings
  • Read News briefings and stories curated for your account
  • Browse Discover and open chats with organisations that publish a Company Sarah
  • Use Chats and Sarah Network features to follow, subscribe, or interact with entities you consent to
  • Manage your profile, notifications, and subscription options where offered

This policy describes how we handle your information as a Sarah consumer. It does not describe how a school, dealership, or other business processes its own customer data inside the Company Portal.

3. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to operate Sarah, improve reliability, and provide the features you use.

3.1 Account and profile (Firebase Authentication)

  • Email address, display name, and profile photo you provide
  • Phone number if you choose phone sign-in or add it to your profile
  • Firebase user identifiers, session tokens, and authentication logs
  • Consumer subscription tier, plan metadata, and billing references where you purchase a paid plan (payment card details are handled by our payment provider, not stored in full on our servers)

3.2 Personal Sarah and Work conversations

  • Messages, prompts, and attachments you send to Sarah
  • AI-generated replies, tool results, and conversation metadata (timestamps, thread identifiers)
  • Work-mode tasks, approvals, and artefacts you create when you use Work features

Message content is processed by automated systems, including large-language-model providers, to generate responses. Limited authorised personnel may access conversation data for security, abuse prevention, support, or quality review under confidentiality obligations.

3.3 My Knowledge

  • Text, notes, and structured fields you save in My Knowledge categories
  • Documents or files you upload for personal reference, where the feature is enabled
  • Category-level permission and sharing settings you configure (including what Sarah may use in replies)

You control what you store. Sarah uses permitted sections to personalise your experience; content you mark as private or exclude from use must not be used for replies outside your settings.

3.4 News

  • Stories and editions you open, read, or save
  • Preferences that shape your briefing (topics, locale, or dismiss actions where available)

3.5 Discover and company chats

  • Businesses or listings you view, search for, or open from Discover
  • Messages you send in a company Sarah chat thread (shared with that organisation’s workspace)
  • Booking, enquiry, or form data you submit when a business enables those tools

3.6 Sarah Network and social features

  • Follow, subscribe, or connection choices you make with Sarah Network entities (including SN-ID relationships)
  • Consent records where a business or network feature asks for permission to contact you or use your data
  • Status or feed interactions where you post or comment in consumer network features

3.7 Notifications and device data

  • Push notification tokens (for example Firebase Cloud Messaging) when you enable notifications
  • Device type, operating system, app version, and coarse technical logs for crashes and performance
  • Approximate location only when you use a location-based Discover feature (for example “near me”); we do not continuously track GPS in the background unless a feature you activate clearly states otherwise

3.8 Usage and service metering

  • Feature usage, API usage, and credit or quota counters tied to your plan
  • Security and audit logs (sign-in events, suspicious activity flags)

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, authenticate, and secure your Sarah account
  • Operate Personal and Work Sarah, including AI-assisted replies and tools you invoke
  • Apply My Knowledge and preferences within the boundaries you set
  • Deliver News, Discover, chats, and Network features
  • Send service, security, and (where permitted) promotional notifications
  • Enforce terms, prevent abuse, and comply with law
  • Improve reliability and develop features using aggregated or de-identified statistics where possible

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your private Personal Sarah chats to train public third-party AI models in a way that identifies you, except as described under service providers below and as required to operate the service you request.

5. AI processing

Sarah uses artificial intelligence to interpret your messages and generate responses. Depending on the feature, processing may include:

  • Routing your prompt and permitted context (including relevant My Knowledge excerpts) to model providers
  • Running tools (for example search, booking helpers, or company-specific capabilities) on your behalf
  • Logging errors or safety flags without unnecessarily retaining sensitive content

AI outputs may be inaccurate; you are responsible for decisions you make based on Sarah’s suggestions, especially for health, legal, financial, or child-related matters. Businesses may apply additional rules in company chats.

6. How we share information

  • Businesses you choose on Discover — messages and enquiry data you send in that business’s chat are available to authorised staff and systems in that organisation’s ZiricAI workspace, subject to their policies.
  • Sarah Network — information you share or consent to share with a network entity (for example subscribing to updates) is shared according to that flow’s notice.
  • Service providers — subprocessors that host, deliver, or support Sarah under contract and confidentiality terms (see section 7).
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, court order, or to protect users, MyPlayc, or others from harm or fraud.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice where required by law.

7. Third-party providers

Sarah relies on established providers, including:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Storage, and related Google Cloud services) — account and app infrastructure
  • OpenAI and similar AI vendors — model inference for chat and tools, subject to API terms and our configuration
  • Hosting and CDN (for example Railway, Netlify, or equivalent) — API and web delivery
  • Payment processors (for example Paystack or Stripe, when enabled) — subscription checkout; we receive confirmation and metadata, not your full card number

These providers process data on our instructions for service delivery. Their own privacy terms may also apply to how they operate their platforms.

8. International transfers

Data may be processed in South Africa and other countries where our providers operate data centres (including the United States and European Union). We use contractual and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data and applicable law, including POPIA where it applies.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, authentication, and monitoring designed to protect Sarah accounts and data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; please use a strong password, protect your device, and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

10. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide Sarah, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may differ by data type (for example billing records, security logs, or company chat copies held in a business workspace).

Deletion and export:

  • You may delete specific My Knowledge content in the app where the feature is available.
  • You may request account closure and deletion of consumer-held personal data by contacting privacy@ziricai.com from your registered email. We will verify your identity and respond within timeframes required by law (typically within 30 days under POPIA).
  • Messages already delivered to a business chat may remain in that business’s records; contact the business directly for their retention practices.

11. Your rights

11.1 POPIA (South Africa)

Where POPIA applies, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal information, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator at www.inforegulator.org.za.

11.2 How to exercise your rights

Email privacy@ziricai.com with the subject line “Sarah App — privacy request”. Include enough detail for us to identify your account. For company-chat data, we may direct you to the relevant business where they are the controller.

12. Children and guardians

Sarah is intended for individuals who can lawfully enter into a service contract under local law (typically 16 years or older in South Africa). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

Some schools use ZiricAI Educare features; guardian or learner flows may involve information processed by the school as controller. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Sarah without consent, contact privacy@ziricai.com.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Sarah, law, or our providers change. We will post the new version at marketing.ziricai.com/sarah-privacy.html and update the “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice (for example in-app or by email) before material changes take effect.
