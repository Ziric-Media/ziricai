Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

ZiricAI is an AI Business Operating System operated by Ziric Media. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using our platform, website, and connected channels such as WhatsApp.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to the ZiricAI website and marketing pages
  • Business customers who create a ZiricAI account and use the Company Portal
  • End customers who interact with a business through ZiricAI-powered channels (for example WhatsApp, web chat, or email)
  • Team members invited to a customer workspace

When a business uses ZiricAI to communicate with its own customers, that business is typically the data controller for its customer conversations and CRM data. Ziric Media acts as a processor on behalf of that business, in line with our customer agreements.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use ZiricAI, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, company name, role, billing details, and authentication credentials
  • Business configuration — AI employee settings, knowledge base documents, automation workflows, branding, and integration settings
  • Conversation data — messages, attachments, channel metadata, timestamps, and assigned team members
  • CRM data — contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, tags, notes, and pipeline information uploaded or created in your workspace
  • Usage and analytics — feature usage, API calls, error logs, performance metrics, and aggregated platform statistics
  • Device and browser data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and referral URLs when you visit our website or portal
  • Support communications — messages you send to our support team or Sarah, our platform assistant

We ask businesses not to upload special-category personal information (such as health or biometric data) unless they have a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards in place.

3. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the ZiricAI platform
  • Authenticate users and enforce workspace access controls
  • Route, store, and display customer conversations in the unified inbox
  • Generate AI-assisted replies, summaries, and automations configured by your organization
  • Process billing, send service notices, and respond to support requests
  • Monitor security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information.

4. WhatsApp and Meta integration

ZiricAI connects to the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. When you enable WhatsApp:

  • Messages sent to and from your business number are transmitted through Meta's infrastructure
  • Meta may process message content and metadata under its own terms and privacy policies
  • We store conversation history in your isolated tenant workspace so your team can manage customer communications
  • Webhook events from Meta are verified and processed securely on our servers

Businesses are responsible for obtaining any required customer consent before initiating WhatsApp conversations, especially for marketing messages, and for complying with Meta's Business Messaging Policy and applicable local laws such as POPIA.

5. AI processing

ZiricAI uses artificial intelligence to power AI Employees, Sarah, knowledge search, and workflow automation. When AI features are used:

  • Relevant conversation context and knowledge base content may be sent to AI model providers (such as OpenAI) to generate responses
  • Each customer workspace is tenant-isolated — your data is not used to train models for other customers
  • AI outputs are generated based on your configured knowledge, policies, and instructions
  • Human team members can review, override, or escalate AI-handled conversations

You can configure escalation rules, approved response boundaries, and data retention settings within your portal.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website and portal use cookies and local storage to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember preferences such as theme and language
  • Measure site performance and diagnose errors
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

Essential cookies are required for the platform to function. Where optional analytics or marketing cookies are used, we will request consent where required by law. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may limit functionality.

7. How we share information

We share information only when necessary:

  • Service providers — cloud hosting, payment processing, email delivery, AI model APIs, and analytics, under contractual confidentiality obligations
  • Integration partners — such as Meta/WhatsApp, when you connect those services
  • Within your organization — team members you authorize to access your workspace
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice where appropriate

8. Data retention

We retain data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you delete content or close an account, we remove or anonymize data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, billing, or security purposes.

Business administrators can configure retention schedules for CRM records and conversation history within their workspace, subject to plan limits and legal obligations.

9. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, tenant isolation, access controls, audit logging, and regular monitoring. No system is completely secure; please use strong passwords, enable team role restrictions, and report suspected incidents to us promptly.

10. International transfers

ZiricAI and our subprocessors may process data in South Africa and other countries where our infrastructure or service providers operate. Where data is transferred across borders, we apply appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law, including POPIA requirements for cross-border transfers.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) or your local data protection authority

Business customers can manage most workspace data directly in the Company Portal. For requests relating to your ZiricAI account or this policy, contact us using the details below. End customers should generally contact the business they interacted with first; we will assist that business as their processor where appropriate.

12. Children's privacy

ZiricAI is a business platform not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of ZiricAI after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
