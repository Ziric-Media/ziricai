Sarah App — Account & data deletion

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Sarah is the ZiricAI consumer app for individuals. It is operated by MyPlayc (Pty) Ltd under the ZiricAI brand. This page explains how you can request deletion of your Sarah account and associated personal data held for the consumer service.

For full privacy terms, see the Sarah App Privacy Policy.

1. How to request deletion

There is no fully automated “delete account” control in the Sarah app at this time. To request closure of your Sarah account and deletion of consumer data we control, contact us by email:

  • Email: privacy@ziricai.com
  • Subject line: Sarah App — account deletion request
  • From: the email address linked to your Sarah / Firebase sign-in (so we can verify you own the account)

In your message, please include:

  • Your full name (as shown on your Sarah profile, if any)
  • The email address you use to sign in to Sarah
  • A clear statement that you want your Sarah consumer account closed and your personal data deleted, subject to the retention notes below

You may also use the same address for access or correction requests; use the subject line Sarah App — privacy request if your request is not limited to deletion.

2. How we verify your request

We must confirm that the request comes from the account holder. We typically verify by:

  • Checking that your email is sent from the registered Sarah account email
  • Asking you to sign in to Sarah again if we cannot match the account, or asking reasonable security questions where needed
  • Confirming your intent before irreversible steps are taken

If we cannot verify ownership, we may decline to process the deletion until verification succeeds, to protect your account from unauthorised deletion.

3. What we aim to delete

After we verify your request, MyPlayc and the ZiricAI team will process deletion of consumer-side personal data associated with your account, which may include:

  • Your Sarah consumer profile and preferences on ZiricAI servers
  • My Knowledge content stored for your account (you may also remove individual sections in the app where that feature is available before requesting full account deletion)
  • Personal Sarah and Work conversation data stored in consumer-scoped systems for your account
  • Consumer metering, subscription metadata, and network connection records tied to your user identifier, where applicable
  • Your Firebase Authentication account for Sarah, once deletion is completed (or disablement where a legal hold applies)

Deletion is carried out manually through an operator-led process aligned with our backend systems. It is not instantaneous from the moment you send email.

4. What may be retained

Some data may be kept after your account is closed, including:

  • Messages you sent to a business — copies may remain in that organisation’s ZiricAI workspace and under their policies. Contact the business directly about their retention and your rights. We cannot delete their controller records on your behalf.
  • Legal, security, and fraud prevention — limited records (for example audit logs, billing evidence, or dispute records) kept only as long as necessary to comply with law, enforce terms, or resolve disputes
  • Backups — residual copies in encrypted backups may persist for a limited period before routine rotation removes them
  • Anonymised or aggregated data — statistics that no longer identify you may be retained for service reliability and planning

Where retention is required, we restrict use to the purpose that justified retention and delete when that purpose ends or the law allows.

5. How long the process takes

We will acknowledge your request and work to complete verified deletion within timeframes required by applicable law. Under POPIA (South Africa), we aim to respond within 30 days of a verified request, unless an extension is permitted and we inform you.

We will tell you when deletion is completed or if we must retain specific items and why.

6. Questions or follow-up

For questions about a deletion request, status, or what was deleted, email privacy@ziricai.com with the same subject line and reference your original request date.

General support (non-deletion): support@ziricai.com
