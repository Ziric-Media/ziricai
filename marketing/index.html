Meet the AI Employee

What if you could hire a digital employee for almost any role?

An AI Employee is a digital worker designed to perform a specific business role. Unlike a basic chatbot, an AI Employee is connected to your organization's knowledge, instructions, workflows, and business systems.

It can communicate with customers, perform tasks, follow processes, and work continuously — while handing complex or sensitive situations to your human team.
