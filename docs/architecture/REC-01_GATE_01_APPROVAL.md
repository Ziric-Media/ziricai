## Locked decisions (confirmed at Gate 1)

- **Pricing strategy:** Affordable launch pricing; capacity to increase as network and audience grow.
- **Billing:** Monthly campaigns with qualified **presentation allowances**, usage tracking, spending controls; no automatic overage without explicit arrangement; no allowance rollover by default.
- **Launch:** Controlled pilot (60–90 days) with selected **verified** organisations before broad network.
- **Platform:** Recommendations is a **shared platform capability** (Personal Sarah, Company Sarah, Network over time) — not Personal-Sarah-only advertising.
- **Principle:** Businesses pay for **visibility and consideration**, not Sarah's endorsement or trust.
